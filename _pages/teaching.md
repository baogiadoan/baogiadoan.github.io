## Undergraduate Computer Science Thesis Supervision

**Supervisor · UNSW Sydney**

I supervise undergraduate computer science students through a major thesis project
completed across three consecutive courses. The sequence takes students from defining
a research or development problem to implementing, evaluating, and communicating a
substantial technical contribution.

- **[Thesis A (COMP4961)](https://www.handbook.unsw.edu.au/undergraduate/courses/2026/COMP4961):** Students review the literature, identify a research or development gap, define the project scope, and plan the technical work for the following stages.
- **[Thesis B (COMP4962)](https://www.handbook.unsw.edu.au/undergraduate/courses/2026/COMP4962):** Students carry out the core research, software, or hardware work proposed in Thesis A, evaluate their progress, and revise the plan for the final stage.
- **[Thesis C (COMP4963)](https://www.handbook.unsw.edu.au/undergraduate/courses/2026/COMP4963):** Students complete the project, evaluate the final results, and communicate their findings through the written thesis and presentation.
